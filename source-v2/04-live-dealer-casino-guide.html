What Is a Live Dealer Casino?

Live dealer casino games stream a real table and human dealer to the player's screen while bets are placed through a digital interface.

Common games

Blackjack, baccarat and roulette are among the most common live dealer formats.

Why players use them

  • Real-time interaction
  • Physical cards and tables
  • Multiple table limits

Streaming quality, table availability and provider reputation can vary substantially between platforms.
