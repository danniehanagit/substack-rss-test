Responsible Gambling Basics: Limits, Breaks and Budgeting

Gambling should be treated as paid entertainment rather than a way to earn reliable income.

Set a fixed budget

Decide in advance how much can be lost without affecting bills, savings or essential spending.

Use time limits

Session limits can reduce impulsive decisions and encourage regular breaks.

Know when to stop

Chasing losses, borrowing to gamble or hiding gambling activity are warning signs that should be taken seriously.
