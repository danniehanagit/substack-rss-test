How to Read Casino Bonus Terms Before You Claim an Offer

Casino bonuses can look attractive at first glance, but the headline percentage or free-spin count rarely tells the whole story.

Check the wagering requirement

A wagering requirement determines how much qualifying play is required before bonus funds or related winnings can usually be withdrawn.

Look at eligible games

Different games may contribute at different rates. Slots may count differently from table or live dealer games.

Review limits

  • Maximum bet while a bonus is active
  • Maximum convertible winnings
  • Minimum deposit
  • Expiry period

Reading these terms before depositing makes it much easier to compare offers on their actual value rather than their marketing headline.
